# Enemy table

## Per-ID properties (PSX.EXE `0x801393B4`)

4 bytes per main-pool ID: `{bank, HP, contact damage, damage table}`. The common enemy init
`FUN_80109080(obj)` copies them into the object (`+0x27`, `+0x47`, `+0x45`, `+0x44`), and also sets
`+0x24` and `+0x2C` from the stage's per-bank tables (see [Sprites.md](Sprites.md)).

These are defaults. Every enemy picks up its bank from here, but some overwrite HP or contact damage in
their own init afterwards. In Redux, in the intro stage, the Bunby Tank (02) was running with 6 HP and
4 contact damage against 1 and 3 here, and the Palm Tree (04) with 4 contact damage against 3. The
Ammoner (07) matched the table exactly. Mets (01) does not call the common init at all.

Names are the editor's (`Const.mainObjInfo`). "Overlays" lists the `STAGExx.BIN` files whose main table
has code for that ID (from [Overlays.md](Overlays.md)).

## Damage tables (PSX.EXE `0x801399BC`)

An array of 42 pointers to 20-byte tables. An object's `+0x44` picks the table, and the weapon's type
(byte `+0x44` of the shot object in the `0x801B12AC` pool) picks the byte. `FUN_80107400` reads it:

| Value | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0x01..0x7F | HP to remove |
| 0x80 | Not affected, the shot passes |
| 0x81 | Deflected (plays sound 7) |
| 0x82 | Hit registered but no damage |

Index `0x0F` is also used by a separate check that is not a normal shot (it runs when `0x801B298A` is set).
I have not mapped the weapon type numbers to weapon names.
